Birthdays appear as all-day events. Add this link as a calendar subscription in your calendar app.

How does it work?

In Outlook: “Add calendar” → “Subscribe from web”. In Apple Calendar: “File” → “New Calendar Subscription”. Paste the link and subscribe.

Your calendar app controls updates and reminders. It must be able to reach the internal address; cloud services from Microsoft or Google may not be able to access it.
